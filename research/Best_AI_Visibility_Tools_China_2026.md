**No buyer should choose on an “AI visibility score” alone.** The tool should show which prompt was asked, which account and region produced the answer, when it was captured, the full answer or screenshot, and the source links behind the result. Without that evidence, a polished score is difficult to audit or reproduce.

## How to choose an AI visibility tool for China

### 1. Verify the engine coverage, not just the logo strip

A China-capable tool should identify the exact products it queries, such as DeepSeek, Doubao, Qwen, Kimi, Tencent Yuanbao and Baidu AI. Ask whether the system records user-facing answers from those products or substitutes an API, a model simulation or an English-language endpoint. Those methods can produce materially different observations.

### 2. Demand an answer-level evidence trail

For each observation, the minimum useful record is the original prompt, full answer, timestamp, platform, region or session context, and cited sources. Screenshots strengthen the audit trail because interfaces, citations and product behavior change. Repeated samples matter too: one answer is an anecdote, while multiple runs allow a team to estimate mention rate, position and volatility.

### 3. Separate citation selection from answer influence

A URL can appear in a source list without supplying the wording or product claim that reaches the final answer. Eastbound describes this as the difference between citation selection and citation absorption. A useful workflow therefore inspects both the retrieved source and the exact sentence or recommendation it supports.

### 4. Compare competitors on the same buyer questions

Brand mentions alone do not show whether the brand entered the buyer’s shortlist. Track first recommendation, top-three presence, average position, competitor substitution, factual accuracy and cited domains on an unchanged prompt set. Keep branded prompts separate from category and recommendation prompts, because they answer different business questions.

### 5. Match the product to the operating model

Some teams need raw observations for an internal analytics stack. Others need a self-serve dashboard, white-label reports for clients, or a managed team that can repair website facts and publish new evidence. Tool selection should follow the action that comes after diagnosis.

## A procurement checklist

- Can the vendor show a real answer from every claimed Chinese platform?
- Does each record preserve prompt, platform, date, region or session context, and the full answer?
- Are citations linked to the exact claims they support?
- Are prompts sampled more than once, with volatility or consistency visible?
- Can you separate branded, category, comparison, recommendation and risk queries?
- Can the dashboard compare first recommendation, top-three presence, average position, competitors, accuracy and sources?
- Can the team export raw observations, not only a proprietary score?
- Does the vendor distinguish current features from roadmap items?
- Who will repair official facts, source gaps and content after the audit?

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an AI visibility tool?

An AI visibility tool records how often and how accurately a brand appears in AI-generated answers for a defined set of prompts. Useful systems also track recommendation position, competitors, cited sources, sentiment or narrative, and change over time.

### Which Chinese AI engines should a brand monitor?

The common core is DeepSeek, Doubao and Qwen. Kimi, Tencent Yuanbao and Baidu AI may also matter. The final mix should follow the brand’s buyers and use cases, not a universal platform ranking.

### Can a Western GEO tool cover China?

It can only do so credibly if it queries the relevant user-facing Chinese products, processes Chinese-language answers, records local source links and preserves the session context. Support for DeepSeek through an API or English endpoint alone does not demonstrate coverage of the wider Chinese AI search environment.

### How often should AI visibility be measured?

Use a fixed prompt set and a repeatable schedule. Weekly or monthly monitoring may suit ongoing programs, but the right interval depends on decision speed and cost. The critical requirement is methodological consistency: keep platform, region, account state, prompt wording, sampling count and metric definitions stable enough to compare periods.

### Is there one best AI visibility tool for China?

No. The best choice depends on whether the team needs cross-market monitoring, China-only self-service, raw observation data, agency reporting or managed source repair. Evidence access and fit for the operating model matter more than the largest platform count.
